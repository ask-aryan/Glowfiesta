Event Overview

The Riddle-Based Team Treasure Hunt is an interactive team-building activity in which employees work together to solve a series of riddles and clues to discover a hidden treasure. Each solved riddle directs a team to a designated office location or object, where the next clue awaits. The activity focuses exclusively on riddles, logical reasoning, observation, communication, and teamwork — without physical challenges, memory games, or construction activities.

Team Format
Activity Format

Employees are divided into 4–6 teams. Each team follows its own sequence of riddles placed only in approved office areas. Teams receive a starting riddle, solve each clue to find the next location, and continue until they reach the final treasure.

Game Structure
🧩
Stage 01

The Starting Riddle

Kick-off · First clue
  • Each team receives its own starting riddle.
  • The answer points to the first approved location or object.
  • Teams should discuss the clue and agree on an answer before proceeding.
🔎 Begin the hunt
🗺️
Stage 02

Follow the Clues

Sequential riddles · Team collaboration
  • At each approved location, teams find the next riddle card.
  • Each answer leads to the next location in the trail.
  • Clues will be arranged to avoid crowding and interference between teams.
🤝 Think together, move forward
🧠
Stage 03

The Tricky Riddle

Higher difficulty · Careful reasoning
  • Teams encounter a more challenging riddle requiring careful interpretation and logical reasoning.
  • Hints may be requested from the organizer, subject to a time penalty.
  • The organizer verifies progress if a clue or answer is disputed.
💡 Every detail matters
🏆
Stage 04

The Final Clue & Treasure Reveal

Finish line · Winner confirmation
  • The final riddle points to the hidden treasure or its designated location.
  • Teams must provide the correct answer and notify the organizer to validate.
  • The first team to complete the full clue sequence correctly, after any penalties, wins.
🎉 First valid finish wins
Rules & Guidelines
  1. Teams must solve riddles collaboratively and follow the clue sequence.
  2. All clues will be located within the designated office premises.
  3. Participants must not enter restricted areas or disrupt normal office operations.
  4. Clues intended for other teams must not be removed, hidden, or altered.
  5. Running and unsafe behaviour are not permitted.
  6. Hints may be provided by the organizer; a time penalty may apply.
  7. The treasure must not be moved or opened before the activity officially begins.
  8. The organizer's decision will be final in case of any dispute.
Materials Required
Printed riddle and clue cards
Envelopes for clue placement
Team identification cards
Pens and answer sheets
Timer or stopwatch
Treasure box or gift bag
Small prizes for the winning team
Organizer's answer key and location checklist
Winner Selection
Winning Criteria

The winner is the team that correctly solves all required riddles and reaches the final treasure location first. Any hint-related time penalty will be applied before confirming the result. If teams finish with the same adjusted time, a tie-breaker riddle determines the winner.
