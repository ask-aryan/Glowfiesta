Round Structure
💻
Round 01
Online Quiz — Qualifier
Google Form · Quiz App · Open to All
  • Quiz link is shared with all participants at 5:30 PM sharp.
  • Participants answer questions individually on their own devices (phone / laptop).
  • Platform: Google Form or a dedicated quiz app — to be confirmed by quiz owners.
  • Submissions are locked at the cutoff; answers are auto-scored.
  • The top 20 scorers are shortlisted and announced publicly.
🏆 Top 20 advance to the live finals
🔔
Round 02
Buzzer Round
Fastest Finger First · Team-Based · Elimination
  • Top 20 finalists gather at the Reception Area (Ground Floor).
  • Randomly divided into 5 teams of 4 — draw done on the spot.
  • Questions read aloud; first team to buzz in gets to answer.
  • Correct answer earns points; wrong answer may pass to the next team.
  • After all questions: the 3 lowest-scoring teams are eliminated.
  • The top 2 teams advance to the Final — Round 3.
⚡ Only the top 2 teams survive to the Final
🚀
Round 03
Rapid Fire — Grand Final
Top 2 Teams · High Stakes · Timed
  • The two surviving teams go head-to-head in a rapid-fire question battle.
  • Covers varied topics — general knowledge, Diwali, pop culture, company trivia.
  • Team with the highest score at the end wins GlowFiesta Quiz 🏆.
🔥 Highest score takes the crown
⚖️  Tiebreaker Options — If Scores are Equal
Sudden Death Question
One question is asked; the team that answers correctly first wins outright.
