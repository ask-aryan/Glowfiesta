About Glowfiesta
Glowfiesta is our office Diwali celebration, running for four days from 2 to 5 November with five activities. Every activity is run by a small owner team. This playbook tells each owner team what is expected, when, and how to plan.

Do's & Don'ts
✅  Do
  • Start on time — 5:30 PM (Main Day at 12:00 PM).
  • Keep rules simple and share them clearly before play begins.
  • Test every tool, mic and screen before participants arrive.
  • Keep the activity inclusive so that everyone can take part.
  • Tell the coordinator early if you need help, budget or space.
❌  Don't
  • Change the format on the day without informing the coordinator.
  • Let one round or game run over its planned time.
  • Use content that could offend anyone.
  • Leave setup or clean-up to the last minute.
